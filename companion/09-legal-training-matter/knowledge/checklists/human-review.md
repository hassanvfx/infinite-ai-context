# Human review checklist

- [ ] Material is fictional training content and appears in the source register.
- [ ] Each summary statement has a source ID and available locator.
- [ ] Stated dates, names, quantities, negations, and quotations match the source.
- [ ] Unknowns remain open questions.
- [ ] No advice, analysis, conclusion, prediction, recommendation, or real data appears.
- [ ] Journal records the specific review and next safe step.
