# Infinite AI Context Companion

The public companion for *Infinite AI Context: ClineFlow and Google's Open
Knowledge Format* by Hassan Uriostegui, published by Waken AI Labs.

Start at the [companion site](https://hassanvfx.github.io/infinite-ai-context/).
Each numbered lab includes prompts, starter material, an example knowledge
bundle, verification guidance, and a durable ClineFlow journal.

The examples are synthetic and local-first. Do not place credentials, private
files, client documents, legal matter, or personal financial data in a prompt.
The legal lab is educational only and is not legal advice.
